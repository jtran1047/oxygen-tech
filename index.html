About Oxygen Technologies

IT Services & AI Consulting for Australian Businesses

AI & Artificial Intelligence

Oxygen Technologies is one of Australia's leading artificial intelligence and AI consulting firms. We help Australian businesses deploy AI, machine learning, automation and generative AI solutions including Azure OpenAI, Microsoft Copilot, agentic AI systems and robotic process automation (RPA). Our AI team specialises in making artificial intelligence practical, governed and production-ready for Australian enterprises.

Public Cloud & IT Managed Services

As an Australian IT managed services and cloud consulting company, we deliver end-to-end solutions across all major public cloud platforms including Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services (AWS), Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI). Our IT consulting services span cloud strategy, migration, DevOps, security and 24/7 managed services for Australian businesses of all sizes.

Microsoft 365, Data & Modern Workplace

Oxygen Technologies delivers comprehensive Microsoft 365 and M365 services including Microsoft Copilot, Teams, SharePoint, Intune and modern workplace transformation. Our enterprise data and data analytics practice builds modern data platforms, lakehouse architectures and business intelligence solutions. We help Australian organisations unlock the value of their data through enterprise data platforms, self-service analytics and real-time data analytics.
